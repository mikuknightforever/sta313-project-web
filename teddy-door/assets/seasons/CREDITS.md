# Seasonal marks

The spring petal, autumn maple leaf and winter snowflake adapt graphics from Twemoji, copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors. The artwork is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The summer mark is an original drawing of a green, palmately lobed Chinese parasol tree leaf. | Winter | [Snowflake](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/blob/7a3dad4a4da30497093dab22eafba135f02308e1/assets/svg/2744.svg) | Recolored the three intersecting paths to icy white. |
